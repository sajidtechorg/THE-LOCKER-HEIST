-------------- ABOUT THE GAME --------------

A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the party, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in
the Study Room was robbed. The locker contained valuable
documents and assets. Eight people were present in the
cottage that night and one of them is the CULPRIT.

The owner's Personal Assistant has hired you as a private
detective, to investigate the case.

-------------- YOUR MISSION --------------

You must identify the culprit before your credibility
runs out. To do this, you will:

  1. Visit seven locations in the cottage. Each location
     gives you one letter tile and its board position.

  2. Interview eight people (including the victim). Each
     person gives you one letter tile and its board
     position.

  3. Place every letter on the Scrabble board at its
     given position.

  4. When both rows are complete, form two words.

  5. The two words together point to the culprit's role.

    investigation immediately.

Good luck, Detective.
